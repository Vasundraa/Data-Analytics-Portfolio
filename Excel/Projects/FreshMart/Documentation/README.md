# FreshMart Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

## Overview

The **FreshMart Sales & Business Performance Dashboard** is an
interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to give management a
consolidated view of sales performance, profitability, trends, and major
business drivers.

The project follows the FreshMart Business Requirements Document (BRD),
whose central requirement is to connect sales activity with
profitability and operational context rather than evaluating performance
from revenue alone.

## Business Problem

FreshMart management does not have a single consolidated view of
business performance. Information is distributed across multiple
datasets, making it difficult to determine which stores, products,
categories, and sales channels are contributing to profitable growth and
which areas require attention.

A key business concern is that **high sales do not necessarily mean high
profitability**. Discounts, costs, and returns can affect the profit
generated by a store or product.

## Business Objective

The objective of the dashboard is to:

-   Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
-   Compare performance across key business dimensions.
-   Identify major sales and profitability drivers.
-   Highlight areas requiring management attention.
-   Support interactive, data-driven investigation through Excel
    slicers.

## Analytical Approach

The project followed an end-to-end analytical workflow:

1.  Understand the business requirements and stakeholder questions.
2.  Inspect the available FreshMart datasets.
3.  Prepare and consolidate relevant data using Excel / Power Query.
4.  Connect related data using common identifiers.
5.  Enrich the sales transaction data with relevant product and store
    attributes.
6.  Create KPI calculations.
7.  Build PivotTables and PivotCharts for comparative analysis.
8.  Add interactive slicers.
9.  Validate calculations and identify data-quality considerations.
10. Translate the analysis into business insights and management
    recommendations.

## Data Used in the Final Dashboard

The BRD identifies the following analytical datasets:

-   FreshMart Sales Transactions --- 2,500 records
-   FreshMart Products --- 150 records
-   FreshMart Stores --- 180 records
-   FreshMart Customers --- 700 records
-   FreshMart Employees --- 350 records
-   FreshMart Suppliers --- 85 records

The final dashboard analysis uses the sales transaction data enriched
with relevant **Product** and **Store** attributes. The BRD-supported
relationships include:

-   Sales Transactions → Products using `Product_ID`
-   Sales Transactions → Stores using `Store_ID`
-   Sales Transactions → Customers using `Customer_ID`
-   Sales Transactions → Employees using `Employee_ID`
-   Products → Suppliers using `Supplier_ID`
-   Employees → Stores using `Store_ID`

The dashboard contains four slicers:

-   **Return Status**
-   **Product Category**
-   **Month**
-   **Store Region**

All dashboard PivotTables/PivotCharts are connected to the slicers so
management can investigate performance under different filters.

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Overall Performance

The dashboard reports:

-   Revenue: **₹2,894,662.90**
-   Profit: **₹741,797.12**
-   Profit Margin: **25.63%**
-   Quantity Sold: **9,823**
-   Transaction Rows: **2,500**

The dashboard therefore provides the executive-level view requested by
the BRD.

### 2. Monthly Performance

The monthly trend visuals show how transaction activity, revenue, and
profit change over time.

The highest-revenue month in the workbook is **Oct 2025**, with revenue
of **₹447,130.54** and profit of **₹115,858.42**.

The highest transaction-volume month is **Oct 2025**, with **372
transaction rows**.

The lowest transaction-volume month is **Mar 2025**, with **1
transaction rows**.

### 3. Store Performance

The Store Performance chart compares the top stores by sales and profit.

The highest-sales store in the displayed Top 10 is **FreshMart Ahmedabad
Complex**, generating **₹46,791.24** in sales and **₹13,302.19** in
profit.

The Region slicer allows management to narrow the view and investigate
store performance within East, North, South, or West.

### 4. Product Performance

The Top 10 Products chart compares Sales Amount and Profit for the
highest-selling products.

This directly addresses the BRD question:

> **Are the highest-selling products also the most profitable
> products?**

The comparison demonstrates why revenue alone is insufficient for
evaluating product performance.

### 5. Category Performance

**Beverages** is the highest-revenue category at **₹482,999.39**.

However, **Bakery** generates the highest total profit at
**₹149,305.74**, with an approximate margin of **40.88%**.

This difference is particularly important because it shows that the
category with the highest sales is not necessarily the category
generating the highest total profit.

### 6. Sales Channel Performance

Revenue is concentrated in **In-Store**, which contributes approximately
**69%** of total revenue.

Channel revenue shares are:

-   In-Store: **69%**
-   Online: **21%**
-   Mobile App: **10%**

This addresses the BRD requirement to compare performance across sales
channels.

### 7. Discount & Profitability

The discount analysis compares 0%, 5%, 10%, 15%, and 20% discount
levels.

The 0% discount group has an aggregated profit margin of approximately
**30.88%**, compared with approximately **14.79%** for the 20% discount
group.

The data therefore shows an **association between higher discount levels
and lower aggregated profit margin** in this dataset.

This should be treated as a pattern for investigation, **not as proof
that discounts cause lower profitability**, consistent with the BRD
limitation on causal claims.

### 8. Return Status

The Return Status slicer allows management to filter returned versus
non-returned transactions.

In the current transaction rows:

-   Returned: **114 rows** (4.56%)
-   Not Returned: **2,386 rows** (95.44%)

The dashboard uses the slicer for investigation rather than presenting a
dedicated Return Rate KPI.

## Key Business Insights

1.  **Overall performance:** FreshMart generated approximately ₹28.95
    lakh in revenue and ₹7.42 lakh in profit, with a 25.63% profit
    margin.
2.  **Channel concentration:** In-Store is the dominant revenue channel
    at approximately 69%.
3.  **Category difference:** Beverages leads revenue, while Bakery leads
    total profit, demonstrating the importance of evaluating
    profitability separately from sales.
4.  **Product profitability:** The Top 10 Product comparison shows that
    high sales and high profit should be evaluated together.
5.  **Discount pattern:** Aggregated profit margin is lower at 20%
    discount than at 0% discount; this is an association that merits
    further investigation.
6.  **Store performance:** The Store chart highlights the
    strongest-selling stores and their corresponding profit
    contribution.
7.  **Returns:** Returned transaction rows represent approximately 4.56%
    of transaction rows and can be investigated through the Return
    Status slicer.

## Data Quality & Validation Notes

The BRD requires validation of duplicates, missing values, data types,
key fields, and logical validity of measures.

The submitted workbook contains **2,500 transaction rows but 2,490
unique Invoice_Number values**, meaning **10 duplicate Invoice_Number
occurrences** are present.

Therefore:

-   The dashboard's displayed transaction KPI of 2,500 represents
    transaction rows.
-   A unique-invoice transaction count would be 2,490.
-   The intended business definition of Invoice_Number should be
    confirmed before using it as the formal transaction-count basis.

There are also **4 missing Store_Name values and 4 missing Store_City
values** in the enriched data. These should be investigated before any
store/city-level completeness claim.

These notes are consistent with the BRD requirement to validate
Invoice_Number uniqueness and investigate missing values before formal
KPI conclusions.

## Limitations

The current dashboard does not include dedicated visual analysis for:

-   Customer/membership contribution
-   Employee contribution
-   Monthly target achievement
-   Payment modes
-   Day types
-   Order times
-   Dedicated Return Rate KPI
-   Full brand/subcategory analysis

The BRD also states that Monthly_Target alignment must be validated
before formal target conclusions, Return_Status definitions should be
reviewed before calculating return KPIs, and the analysis should
identify associations rather than claim causation.

## Recommendations / Areas for Further Investigation

-   Validate and resolve duplicate Invoice_Number occurrences before
    finalizing the formal transaction KPI definition.
-   Investigate missing Store_Name / Store_City values.
-   Review stores with comparatively lower profit relative to sales.
-   Investigate discounting policies, especially higher discount levels
    and their relationship with profitability.
-   Review high-revenue categories/products that have comparatively
    lower profitability.
-   Extend the dashboard with customer/membership and employee analysis
    if broader BRD coverage is required.
-   Validate Monthly_Target period alignment and add target-vs-actual
    analysis where appropriate.
-   Add a dedicated Return Rate or return-pattern visual after
    confirming Return_Status definitions.
